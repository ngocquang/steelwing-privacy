Privacy Policy — Steel Wing

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Steel Wing (bundle ID com.qgame.steelwing) is a single-player arcade game. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics or advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We never see your device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or usage data.

Data stored on your device

The game saves two things locally on your device so it can remember your progress:

  • Your best score.
  • Your audio settings (music and sound-effect volume, mute state).

This information never leaves your device and is not readable by us. Deleting the app removes it.

Network access

All game assets ship inside the app. Steel Wing does not send data to any server we operate.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data at all, it collects none from children. It complies with COPPA and the GDPR by design.

Third parties

We share nothing with third parties, because we hold nothing to share.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date above.
