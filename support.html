Common questions

There is no sound.

Check the ring/silent switch on the side of your iPhone and the volume level. You can also adjust music and sound effects in the game's settings.

My best score disappeared.

Your best score and audio settings are stored on your device only. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears them, and they do not transfer between devices.

The game runs slowly or the screen stutters.

Close other apps running in the background and restart Steel Wing. If it persists, email us with your device model — it helps us tune performance.

How do I play?

Steer your craft by dragging on the screen; firing is automatic. Survive the waves, clear the boss, and beat your best score.
